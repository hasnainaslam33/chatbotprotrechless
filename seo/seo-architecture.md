# SEO Architecture

## Recommended structure

Main website:

- protrenchless.com
- Purpose: ranking, trust, local SEO, service pages, booking, conversion

AI center subdomain:

- answers.protrenchless.com
- Purpose: tools, guided diagnosis, second opinions, estimate reviews, camera/video review intake, education, and lead qualification

## Index these pages

- AI Sewer Decision Center homepage
- Symptom checker landing page
- Estimate comparison landing page
- Camera video review landing page
- Realtor sewer risk landing page
- Property manager drain triage landing page
- Restaurant drain risk landing page
- Home inspector sewer scope support
- Commercial sewer risk review

## Noindex these

- Individual AI chat transcripts
- User-upload pages
- Thin generated answers
- Duplicate AI outputs
- Private reports
- Low-quality auto-generated pages
- Internal admin routes

## Main-site internal links

Every result should link back to the most relevant money page on protrenchless.com.

Use descriptive anchor text:

- Learn more about sewer camera inspections in Southeastern Pennsylvania.
- Compare trenchless sewer repair and excavation.
- Schedule a Pro Trenchless sewer inspection.
- Review sewer repair options before approving an estimate.

Do not use generic anchor text like “click here.”

## Content feedback loop

Use anonymized questions from the AI tool to create expert-reviewed main-site pages, such as:

- Why does my basement drain back up when I run the washing machine?
- Should I hydro jet before replacing my sewer line?
- Is pipe bursting better than digging up my yard?
- What does an offset sewer joint mean?
- Should a realtor require a sewer camera inspection?

Publish only reviewed, original, useful content on the main website.
